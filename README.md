# Cyber Threat Intelligence Portfolio

Welcome to my Cyber Threat Intelligence portfolio. This repository is a collection of hands-on CTI work showing how I analyze threat actors, map adversary behavior to MITRE ATT&CK, review indicators of compromise, conduct OSINT research, and turn threat data into actionable intelligence for defenders.

My goal is simple: take raw threat information and answer the questions that matter most to security teams:

* Are we affected?
* What activity should we care about?
* What TTPs are being used?
* What detections or response actions should we prioritize?
* How do we turn intelligence into action?

## About Me

I am a cybersecurity professional with experience in Cyber Threat Intelligence, SOC operations, EDR/SIEM workflows, incident response support, threat hunting, and detection engineering. My background includes working with enterprise security teams to analyze adversary behavior, improve detection coverage, and communicate technical risk in a way that helps leaders and defenders make decisions.

This portfolio is built to demonstrate practical CTI skills, not just theory.
