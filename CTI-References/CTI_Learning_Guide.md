# Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) Learning Guide

Resources organized by level: Books, Videos, and Free Training.

---

## 🟢 Beginner

### Books

**Threat Intelligence for the Complete Beginner**
Demystifies cyber threats and how defenders use intelligence to stop them. Great first read.

**The Threat Intelligence Handbook — CrowdStrike**
Practical guide to unlocking the power of intelligence for security teams. Widely referenced in the field.
> — https://www.crowdstrike.com/resources/reports/threat-intelligence-handbook/

**NIST SP 800-150 — Guide to Cyber Threat Information Sharing**
The US government's authoritative framework for how threat intelligence fits into organizational security programs.
> — https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/detail/sp/800-150/final

### Videos

**Hak5 — ThreatWire (YouTube)**
Weekly series covering current security threats, threat actor news, and intelligence trends. Running since 2012, it's one of the longest-standing free threat intelligence news shows in the infosec community. Great for staying current as a beginner.
>  — https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLW5y1tjAOzI0Sx4UU2fncEwQ9BQLr5Vlu

**John Hammond — Cyber Threat Intelligence Intro (YouTube)**
Practical channel covering SOC and CTI concepts in digestible, real-world scenarios.
> — https://www.youtube.com/@_JohnHammond

### Free Training

**arcX — Cyber Threat Intelligence 101**
Best free starting point. Covers the intelligence lifecycle, collection, and the CTI analyst role. Self-paced with a certificate.
>  | Certificate — https://arcx.io/courses/cyber-threat-intelligence-101

**Cybrary — Intro to Cyber Threat Intelligence**
2–3 hour free course covering CTI fundamentals, major frameworks, and the CTI analyst's role in a security team.
>  — https://www.cybrary.it

**EC-Council — Free CTI Foundations Course**
Covers the threat intelligence lifecycle, data vs. intelligence, and how CTI supports SOC operations.
>  | Certificate — https://www.eccouncil.org/cybersecurity-exchange/cyber-novice/free-cybersecurity-courses-beginners/

**MITRE ATT&CK Navigator (Interactive Tool)**
Not a course, but 30 focused minutes here teaches you more about real attack structure than most lectures. Free forever.
>  — https://mitre-attack.github.io/attack-navigator/

## 🟡 Intermediate

### Books

**Mastering Cyber Intelligence — Jean-Philippe Tiranno (Packt)**
Covers CTI tradecraft, standards, threat modeling, and integration with SOC/SIEM. Solid bridge from beginner to practitioner.
> 

**Practical Threat Intelligence and Data-Driven Threat Hunting — Valentina Palacín**
Hands-on guide combining CTI and threat hunting with the ATT&CK framework. Uses real-world malware examples.
> 

**Cybersecurity Attack and Defense Strategies — Yuri Diogenes & Erdal Ozkaya**
Covers red/blue team dynamics, threat intelligence integration, incident response, and detection rule development.
>

### Videos

**MITRE ATT&CK CTI Training — Official (Video Series)**
5-module official training from the ATT&CK team. ~4 hours. Covers how to apply ATT&CK specifically to CTI workflows.
>  — https://attack.mitre.org/resources/learn-more-about-attack/training/cti/

**SANS CTI Summit Talks (YouTube)**
Annual summit recordings covering threat actor tracking, intelligence operationalization, and analyst tradecraft.
>  — https://www.youtube.com/@SANSInstitute

### Free Training

**Cybrary — Application of MITRE ATT&CK Framework**
Walks through all 14 ATT&CK tactic categories with mitigation strategies. Covers blue team application and detection mapping.
>  — https://www.cybrary.it/course/application-of-the-mitre-attack-framework

## 🔴 Expert

### Books

**Intelligence-Driven Incident Response, 2nd Ed. — Brown & Roberts (O'Reilly)**
The gold standard for applying intelligence tradecraft to incident response using the F3EAD cycle. Essential for senior analysts.
> 

**Structured Analytic Techniques for Intelligence Analysis — Heuer & Pherson**
66 structured analytic techniques used by the intelligence community. Covers Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH) and bias mitigation.
> 

**Psychology of Intelligence Analysis — Richards Heuer (CIA)**
How analysts think, where cognitive bias enters the process, and how to build analytical rigor.
>  — https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/books-monographs/psychology-of-intelligence-analysis-2/

**Cyber Threat Intelligence: A Hands-On Guide (Rheinwerk Computing)**
Deep dive into the full intelligence lifecycle — OSINT to SIGINT, threat modeling, MISP/OpenCTI automation, custom detection logic, and real-world workflows.
>
